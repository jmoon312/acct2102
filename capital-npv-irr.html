Step 4

Price in a possible Year-3 failure

building the decision on expected NPV

Capital budgeting looks forward, so it has to plan for what might happen. Ramble's engineers estimate a 50% chance that a critical drum on the roasting line fails at the end of Year 3 and has to be replaced — a one-time $35,000 cash outlay on top of that year's normal operations. The other 50% of the time the line runs clean and the cash flows are exactly the ones you discounted in Step 2.

First find the NPV assuming the replacement happens: only Year 3 moves, so keep the Year 1, 2, and 4 present values from Step 2 and rework Year 3. Then weight the two scenarios by their probabilities to get the expected NPV.
